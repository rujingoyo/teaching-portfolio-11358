## Inclusive audit

My inclusive audit examines the Year 10 Mathematics digital citizenship activity developed for Assessment 2A. Existing headings, success criteria and teacher-provided AI output offer a useful foundation. The audit identified potential barriers in explanatory prose, specialised vocabulary and unspecified response options.

Universal Design for Learning guides the audit through Engagement, Representation, and Action and Expression. [Phelan et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejed.70016) identify these principles across secondary subjects, highlighting approaches that support participation through varied materials, scaffolding and opportunities to communicate understanding.

- For Engagement, I made students’ choice of starting audit prompt explicit and provided individual thinking time before discussion.
- For Representation, I divided instructions into manageable sections, clarified algebraic vocabulary and placed an expandable transcript below the walkthrough video.
- For Action and Expression, students can now submit annotated working, a typed explanation, audio explanation or equivalent method using the same mathematical criteria.


These changes address more than access to multimedia. Iterative design enables me to revisit assumptions about how students access and demonstrate learning. Student feedback and classroom observations will inform further adjustments, while retaining the activity’s mathematical challenge.

| UDL principle | Barrier identified in 2A | Implemented modification | Before-and-after evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engagement** | Four audit dimensions are provided, but choice of starting point and preparation time are not explicit. | Added a choice of starting question and one minute of individual thinking time. | [Before](assets/inclusive-audit/digital-citizenship-before.md#audit) · [After](digital-citizenship.md#audit) |
| **Representation** | Explanatory prose and terms such as *discriminant* and *factorise* may increase reading and processing demands. | Split explanatory and safety text into bullets; added optional vocabulary explanations and a hint. | [Before](assets/inclusive-audit/digital-citizenship-before.md#why-audit-ai) · [After](digital-citizenship.md#why-audit-ai) · [Optional help](digital-citizenship.md#audit) |
| **Representation** | The walkthrough transcript requires navigation to a separate page. | Added an expandable transcript directly beneath the video. | [Before](assets/inclusive-audit/professional-learning-before.md#design-rationale-video) · [After](professional-learning.md#design-rationale-video) |
| **Action and Expression** | “Annotate the response” does not specify alternative ways to demonstrate reasoning. | Added annotations, text, audio or an agreed equivalent through the school-approved platform; retained common success criteria. | [Before](assets/inclusive-audit/digital-citizenship-before.md#audit) · [After](digital-citizenship.md#audit) |
