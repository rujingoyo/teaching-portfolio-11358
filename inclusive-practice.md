## Inclusive audit

My inclusive audit examines the Year 10 Mathematics digital citizenship activity developed for Assessment 2A. Existing headings, success criteria and teacher-provided AI output offer a useful foundation. However, explanatory prose, specialised vocabulary and unspecified response options may create barriers for some learners.

Universal Design for Learning guides the audit through Engagement, Representation, and Action and Expression. [Phelan et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejed.70016) identify these principles across secondary subjects, highlighting approaches that support participation through varied materials, scaffolding and opportunities to communicate understanding.

- For Engagement, I will make students’ choice of starting audit prompt explicit and provide individual thinking time before discussion.
- For Representation, I will divide instructions into manageable sections, clarify algebraic vocabulary and place a transcript beside the walkthrough video.
- For Action and Expression, students will be able to submit annotated working, a typed explanation, audio explanation or equivalent method using the same mathematical criteria.


These changes address more than access to multimedia. Iterative design enables me to revisit assumptions about how students access and demonstrate learning. Student feedback and classroom observations will inform further adjustments, while retaining the activity’s mathematical challenge.

## Audit and retrofit log

|---|---|---|---|
| **Engagement** | Four audit dimensions are provided, but choice of starting point and preparation time are not explicit. | Add a choice of starting prompt and individual thinking time before discussion. | Before-and-after screenshots of the participation instructions. |
| **Representation** | Explanatory prose and terms such as *discriminant* and *factorise* may increase reading and processing demands. | Present instructions as short stages; provide optional vocabulary explanations and hints. | Before-and-after screenshots showing the staged instructions and supports. |
| **Representation** | The walkthrough transcript requires navigation to a separate page. | Add an expandable transcript directly beneath the video. | Screenshots showing the original transcript link and the expanded transcript beside the media. |
| **Action and Expression** | “Annotate the response” does not specify alternative ways to demonstrate reasoning. | Allow annotated working, typed explanations or brief audio explanations through a school-approved platform; retain common success criteria. | Before-and-after screenshots showing response choices and assessment criteria. |
