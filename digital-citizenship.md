**Year 10 Mathematics | Ethical AI use | AC9M10A01**

## Learning intention:

I can evaluate whether an AI-generated algebra solution is correct, purposeful, explainable and efficient.

## Success criteria:

I can

- verify each algebraic step independently
- justify which method I would choose
- explain how I used AI safely and transparently
- produce a more structural solution

## Why audit AI?

While generative AI can produce a correct solution and answer, it often fails to provide the clearest solution or often contains steps that are not required or relevant to the process. Limited verification and uncritical acceptance of output generated by ChatGPT lead to superficial mathematical engagement (Bulut & Borromeo Ferri, 2026). AI output should therefore be analysed, studied, and explained instead of being treated as an authority to copy, as explaining and comparing worked examples can strengthen both procedural and conceptual knowledge (Booth et al., 2013).

## Curriculum connection

Australian Curriculum v9.0 content description AC9M10A01 requires students to expand, factorise and simplify expressions using mathematical laws and properties (ACARA, n.d.). This audit aims to develop the outcome by asking students to recognise algebraic structure, the validity of substitution steps and the verification of equivalent expressions.

## Before Using AI

Check AI regulations and permissions, including which AI models are permitted and which AI-generated materials are allowed by your teacher. Never enter personal information, including names, login credentials, assessment files or another person’s work. Record the tool, date and prompt, and acknowledge output used or changed (Department of Education, 2023).

## Audit this response

### The problem

Positive numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy

$$
x^2+4xy+4y^2-6x-12y=7.
$$

Evaluate $x+2y$.

### AI-assisted response sample

Source: GPT-5, 07/2026. Reformatted.

> I will treat the equation as a quadratic in $x$:
>
> $$
> x^2+\left(4y-6\right)x+\left(4y^2-12y-7\right)=0
> $$
>
> The discriminant is
>
> $$
> \left(4y-6\right)^2-4\left(4y^2-12y-7\right)=64
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> x=\frac{-\left(4y-6\right)\pm8}{2}=7-2y\mathrm{\ or\ }-1-2y
> $$
>
> Because $x$ and $y$ are positive, the second value is impossible. Hence $x+2y=7$.

## Audit

annotate the response

### Correct?

- Can every line be justified?
- Can you replicate each line to obtain the same result?
- How can one use the discriminant to obtain the result?

### Purpose?

- What purpose does it serve to treat the expression as a quadratic in $x$?
- What is the purpose of using the discriminant?

### Explainable?

- Could you reconstruct the solution on your own for a peer?

### Efficient?

- Observe $x^2+4xy+4y^2=\left(x+2y\right)^2$ and $-6x-12y=-6\left(x+2y\right)$
- Could you use fewer calculations by using the above observation?

## Teacher answer

The more efficient method is:

$$
\left(x+2y\right)^2-6\left(x+2y\right)=7
$$

$$
\text{Let }t=x+2y
$$

$$
t^2-6t=7
$$

$$
t^2-6t-7=0
$$

$$
\left(t-7\right)\left(t+1\right)=0
$$

$$
t=x+2y=7>0
$$

Both methods are correct. Which would you choose, and why? Compare their structure, efficiency, explainability and connection to the requested expression.
